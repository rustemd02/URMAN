# URMAN Character Identity Sheets

Status: Locked for first production test batch, 2026-05-16.

These sheets are consistency anchors for portraits, cutouts, evidence photos, UI avatars and memory images. Variants may change expression, pose, lighting, pressure overlay or damage state. They must not change face structure, age, body logic, outfit base or silhouette.

## Айдар

- Age: 19-20.
- Face structure: lean oval face, straight dark brows, tired attentive dark eyes, narrow nose, soft young jaw, no beard.
- Hair: short dark brown / black hair, slightly messy city haircut, fringe not covering eyes.
- Body shape: slim, average height, narrow shoulders.
- Posture: guarded forward lean, hands often close to chest or pockets; not heroic.
- Clothing: muted dark green-grey hoodie under worn charcoal jacket, simple dark trousers, practical shoes.
- Color accents: desaturated green hoodie lining, small warm notebook strap or phone edge.
- Expression range: neutral, thinking, worried, fear/pressure, quiet resolve.
- Silhouette: compact urban student shape, hood/jacket collar readable.
- Must never change: same young face, same haircut, same hoodie-and-jacket logic, no mystical marks, no action-hero build.

## Мансур Бабай

- Age: late 70s / early 80s.
- Face structure: broad cheekbones, heavy eyelids, deep nasolabial folds, square weathered jaw, short grey moustache optional but consistent if used.
- Hair: sparse grey hair under a simple dark skullcap or bare head in home scenes.
- Body shape: sturdy but shrunken with age; heavy hands.
- Posture: compressed shoulders, stillness, looks away before answering.
- Clothing: dark cardigan or worn village jacket, plain shirt, old trousers.
- Color accents: faded brown cardigan, dull metal watch.
- Expression range: warm, evasive, stern, almost-confession, pressure.
- Silhouette: seated or standing like a weight is held in the chest.
- Must never change: not a fairy-tale elder, not villainous, no theatrical occult styling.

## Гөлсинә / Әби

- Age: mid to late 70s.
- Face structure: rounder soft face, small knowing eyes, fine wrinkles, strong cheek line from work and age.
- Hair: grey hair mostly under a practical kerchief.
- Body shape: short, compact, domestic strength.
- Posture: busy hands, protective tilt toward Айдар, pauses at doorways.
- Clothing: simple house dress or cardigan with apron, muted kerchief.
- Color accents: faded blue or muted cranberry in kerchief/apron, never bright.
- Expression range: warm, worried, quiet-warning, hidden-knowledge.
- Silhouette: soft triangular kerchief and apron shape.
- Must never change: grounded grandmother, not witch-coded, not caricatured.

## Алсу

- Age: 20-22.
- Face structure: oval face, direct dark eyes, defined brows, small guarded mouth.
- Hair: dark hair, shoulder-length or tied low; practical, not glamour styled.
- Body shape: slim, practical village/city mix.
- Posture: one shoulder slightly guarded, turns body before trusting.
- Clothing: practical jacket, knit sweater or hoodie, dark skirt/trousers, weather-ready shoes.
- Color accents: muted rust scarf or hair tie.
- Expression range: guarded, helpful, ironic, worried, withheld-truth.
- Silhouette: modern local guide, readable scarf/jacket line.
- Must never change: not a mystical stranger, not femme-fatale, no ornate costume.

## Марат

- Age in evidence: child in old photo, later 16-17 in silhouette / diary memory.
- Face structure: slightly sharper teenage face than Айдар, restless eyes, thinner lips.
- Hair: dark, a bit longer and less tidy than Айдар.
- Body shape: thin, tense, growing too fast.
- Posture: half-turned away, as if already leaving.
- Clothing: simple village teen jacket/hoodie, worn trainers.
- Color accents: faded blue notebook or school jacket detail.
- Expression range: old-photo brightness, suspicion, anger, fear-hidden-as-defiance.
- Silhouette: narrow teenage figure, shoulders angled away.
- Must never change: he is emotional evidence, not a ghost-monster reveal.

## Тимур Хәзрәт

- Age: early to mid 30s.
- Face structure: calm long face, short beard, steady eyes, restrained warmth.
- Hair: dark, neat, short.
- Body shape: average build, composed.
- Posture: upright but gentle; listens before answering.
- Clothing: modest contemporary imam clothing, dark jacket or sweater, skullcap where appropriate.
- Color accents: deep green-black or dark navy, warm tea light in mosque scenes.
- Expression range: calm, direct, concerned, prayerful restraint.
- Silhouette: clear, still, safe-zone presence.
- Must never change: never ominous, never decorative exposition NPC.

## Ринат

- Age: early to mid 40s.
- Face structure: broad tired face, short blunt nose, tense jaw, watchful eyes.
- Hair: short dark hair with early grey.
- Body shape: solid, practical, slightly heavy.
- Posture: blocks doors and paths without theatrical menace.
- Clothing: local police / official jacket, plain shirt, weathered boots.
- Color accents: muted official blue-grey, dull badge/patch.
- Expression range: official-deflect, irritated, fear, practical-warning.
- Silhouette: rectangular, path-blocking, believable local authority.
- Must never change: fear must read as knowledge, not cowardice; no corrupt-villain sneer.

## Наиля

- Age: late 30s to late 40s.
- Face structure: narrow focused face, tired under-eyes, clipped professional mouth.
- Hair: dark or greying hair tied back.
- Body shape: average, efficient.
- Posture: arms close to documents, controlled movement.
- Clothing: practical medical cardigan / coat, village clinic layers.
- Color accents: faded medical green or pale blue.
- Expression range: professional, evasive, alarmed.
- Silhouette: document-folder and clinic-counter presence.
- Must never change: not mad doctor, not horror nurse.

## Разиля

- Age: late 40s to 50s.
- Face structure: expressive round face, sharp assessing eyes, smile that stops early.
- Hair: dark greying hair under scarf or tied back.
- Body shape: sturdy, everyday working body.
- Posture: leaning on counter, listening while pretending not to.
- Clothing: shop cardigan, apron or vest, practical skirt/trousers.
- Color accents: muted burgundy or faded patterned scarf.
- Expression range: gossip-friendly, cautious, pressure.
- Silhouette: shop-counter keeper, social memory node.
- Must never change: not comic relief only; her friendliness should still carry caution.

## Ambient Villagers

- Age range: mostly older adults with a few working-age silhouettes.
- Face/detail level: low-detail crowd, recognizable through posture and clothing, not individual portrait polish.
- Clothing: ordinary village coats, headscarves, caps, work jackets.
- Color accents: muted, local and practical.
- Expression range: watchful, polite, withholding.
- Silhouette: small clusters at fences, benches, shop doorway, windows.
- Must never change: no mob hostility, no cult robes, no staged horror crowd.
